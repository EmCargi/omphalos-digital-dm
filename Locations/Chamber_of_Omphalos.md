# The Chamber of Omphalos — The Seven Thrones

**Type:** The secret chamber — the centre of the Multiverse
**Hook:** The 6-node starter's lore node (`node_04_empty_throne`)

A small, dark chamber. Seven thrones, each unique in shape and design, ring the space. Six are seated — the gatekeepers of the Prime Worlds. The seventh, Earth's, is empty: Ozymandius's seat, the deciding vote, the missing keystone of the Multiverse.

**The Deadlock:** Without Earth's gatekeeper, the Council can't act. Bazaroth's rogue member grows in the vacuum. The party must read the empty throne — and decide who takes it.

**Mechanics:** A `lore` node — reach it, and the party learns the whole game is the deciding vote.
