# The Way-Bound

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 310 (The Anime Multiverse).

## The Way-Bound

Certain individuals are **Way-Bound** — their souls are linked to the Cosmic Web. This means they can be summoned by anyone performing an appropriate ritual at a gate that leads to the Way-Bound's home world. If the Way-Bound is already on the summoner's world, the ritual has no effect.

The only way to summon a Way-Bound individual is with that entity's **true name**. In most cases, the individual simply hears a call and decides whether to come. In some cases they may be compelled — usually requiring some other link (a feather from an angel's wing, a talisman of a demon).

**Powerful demons and asrai (angels) are Way-Bound, as are the gatekeepers of each world.**

## Cast Hook

The gatekeepers of the six Prime Worlds are all Way-Bound — which is how the Council can convene. And a true-name ritual at the chamber could summon — or bind — the missing Earth gatekeeper.
