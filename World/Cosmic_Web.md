# The Cosmic Web — Earth's Waylines

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 309–311 (The Anime Multiverse).

## The Cosmic Web

The Cosmic Web is the set of linked worlds directly connected to Earth — the Multiverse. Waylines are the interdimensional connections between worlds, visualized as a "Cosmic Web" whose strands connect them. The formation appears deliberate: some time in the past, **the Weaver** connected all the strands. Some believe the Weaver was a god; others, powerful archmages. The completion dates back at least 10,000 Earth years.

**Weaver relics** — artefacts of power that exert control over the Cosmic Web — still exist. The most common are the **world gates**.

## Earth's Waylines

Earth is the only world known to possess **eight waylines**:
- Six that connect to the Prime Worlds (Aradia, Bazaroth, Enid, Cathedral, Ikaris, Imago)
- One unusable wayline that detached long ago and floats freely
- One secret wayline that leads to the **Omphalos chamber**

These make Earth the "hub" of the known Multiverse.

## World Gates

Gates are places where a wayline can be entered — a ring of toadstools, a door, a stone circle, an enchanted mirror, a painting, a mountain pool, a well, a tomb, a giant idol's mouth, a "stargate" machine. Anyone who touches a gate and expresses a wish to be elsewhere is transported by it.

## Cast Hook

The demo's arrival — the party reaches the chamber via Earth's secret wayline, the door to Omphalos itself.
