# Keys & Skeleton Keys

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 310 (The Anime Multiverse).

## Keys

Most gates have a **Key** — someone whose soul is especially attuned to that gate or to the world on the other side. According to one theory, a Key lived on the other world in a past life and their soul "crossed over" during reincarnation. Others say a Key is simply someone born near the gate and attuned to it very early.

- Most gates are normally "open," but a **Key can take control** of its gate if it discovers the unique power.
- A Key can **lock** the gate shut, or **open** it if locked.
- If a Key is ritually killed while in contact with their gate, the **gate is destroyed**.
- If a Key dies in other circumstances, the power passes to another — usually a newly born child.

## Skeleton Keys

A rare few Keys are **Skeleton Keys**, with a special bond to all doorways and locks:
- Operate **any world gate** on any dimension.
- Operate physical locks, password locks, computer files, bank cards.
- **Permanently destroy** any world gate by passing through it.
- **Detect nearby** world gates.

Few understand a Skeleton Key's true power; those who do go to great lengths to capture one. Most Skeleton Keys rarely stay in one place — they travel the Cosmic Web.

## Cast Hook

The demo's signature asset — a Council Gate-Key (legal lane) vs a Skeleton Key (the forsaken lane that can destroy gates).
