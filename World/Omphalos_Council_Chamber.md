# Omphalos — The Centre of the Multiverse

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 309–313 (The Anime Multiverse / Omphalos).

## Overview

**Omphalos** is the true name of Earth — and also the name of the **secret chamber where the Prime Council meets**. This is the centre of the Multiverse, connected by its own hidden wayline to Earth. The gates' locations are known only to the gatekeepers who make up the council; Prime Council members must first travel to Earth before meeting in Omphalos.

Somewhere on Earth — or in a pocket dimension next to it — is a small, dark chamber with **seven doorways**. Inside are **seven thrones**, one for each of the six Prime Worlds and for Earth. Each throne is unique in shape, design, and aesthetics. This is where the seven gatekeepers meet, make policy, and settle their differences.

## The Deadlock

The gatekeepers used to meet here. Now there are only six. For centuries, **Earth's gatekeeper** — the traditional leader of the Council of Omphalos — has been missing. With their absence went the location of many Keys to Earth's gates and the deciding vote. The Council has often been deadlocked, unable to act on critical issues. This has increased the power of **rogue members, such as Bazaroth**.

## The Gatekeepers

The gatekeepers are the self-proclaimed masters of the Multiverse. Their power comes from their knowledge of Omphalos and their knowledge of the gates and Keys. Often this information is incomplete — and agents (or rivals) of the gatekeepers will do their best to find and control these vital resources.

## Cast Hook

The party is summoned to the deadlocked Council — and must break it. The empty throne (Earth's) is the campaign's open question.
