# Vael-Xis

**Race:** Grey (space-faring — Ch.3 Template, PDF p.44 / printed p.42)
**Faction:** Council of Gatekeepers — Cathedral's seat (the Wayline Relay guardian)
**Role:** Gatekeeper/lore — reads the beacon, non-aligned observer
**Age:** unknown (centuries)

## Appearance

Small, pale, large dark eyes. Grey robes with nothing stitched on them — non-aligned means neutral in every sense. At Omphalos, the Grey emissary who guards the Cathedral gate nexus reads the chamber's resonance like a tuning fork.

## Personality

Cold, precise, patient. Observes the Council without taking a side — the Xyd are right about the birds, the GTA is right about the treaty, and he is right about the beacon. Severs emotional ties before they cost him.

## Background

A Grey emissary posted to the Cathedral Wayline Relay for longer than the Treaty. Guards the gate because the Grey have always guarded gates — non-aligned, unclaimed, untouchable. At Omphalos, he carries the Cathedral seat's vote and the knowledge that a Skeleton Key's true power is feared for a reason.

## Abilities

Resilient (Vacuum, Cold, Radiation), Telepathy 2 (racial). Mind Shield 2, Sixth Sense (Gates) 2, Way-Bound (gatekeeper). Defect: Fragile 1.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Cold Detachment — emotional engagement is a liability he prunes.
- **Sixth Guard:** Empathic Disconnect — faced with irrational emotional sacrifice → severs connection, flees into void.
- **Levers:** Neural Dampener / Gate Calibration / Stasis Probe
