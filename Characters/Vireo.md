# Vireo

**Race:** Elarad — a talking hawk (~1/10,000 animals on Aradia)
**Faction:** Council of Gatekeepers — Aradia's seat (the Whirl's Companion)
**Role:** Neutral fixer — the Whirl's guardian, Aradia's vote
**Age:** 34

## Appearance

A sleek falcon with storm-bright eyes, one wing marked with the Whirl's birth-sign. At Omphalos, perched on Aradia's throne — the only non-humanoid seat, and often the one that sees the whole room.

## Personality

Loyal, watchful, cryptic. Guards the Whirl gate to Earth; reads the Cosmic Web the way the Oracles read the storm. The deadlock has made him the Council's memory — he remembers how the chamber used to work.

## Background

Posted to the Whirl as its companion-gatekeeper after the College of Emissaries. Knows the lost-angel search, the wayline to Earth, and that a Skeleton Key could lock or shatter the gate. At Omphalos he carries Aradia's vote — and a hawk's eye for the rogue seat's tricks.

## Abilities

Flight (hawk), Sixth Sense (Gates), Supersense (vision), Way-Bound (gatekeeper). Techniques: Evasion, Precise Aim. Skills: Survival 3, Academic 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Gatekeeper's Vigil — the gate is his whole life.
- **Sixth Guard:** Gate-Breach — the Whirl is compromised → he abandons the Council to seal it, even if it costs the vote.
- **Levers:** Winged Warden / Gate-Resonance / Sky-Dive
