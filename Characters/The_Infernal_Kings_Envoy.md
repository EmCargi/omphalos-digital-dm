# The Infernal King's Envoy

**Race:** Hellspawn — the Infernal King's trusted ambassador
**Faction:** Council of Gatekeepers — Bazaroth's seat
**Role:** Contrarian enforcer — the rogue member
**Age:** centuries

## Appearance

Obsidian-horned, blood-light in the eyes, a legion sigil burnt into the chest. Speaks for the Infernal King when the King chooses not to attend — and the King's absence is always a statement.

## Personality

Ruthless, precise, opportunistic. The deadlock is Bazaroth's garden — the deciding-vote vacuum lets the rogue seat maneuver freely. Claims the demons' destiny: they broke the Invaders, they hunt freely across the Web. The other seats' weakness is the point.

## Background

The Infernal King's trusted demon lord, sent to Omphalos as ambassador. Has spent centuries probing weak worlds, sealing pacts, and waiting for the empty throne to give Bazaroth the deciding vote by default. The party's antagonist at the climax.

## Abilities

Psychic Vampire, Shape-shift (minor), Way-Bound (gatekeeper). Techniques: Brutal, Judge Opponent. Skills: Military 3, Occult 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Rogue's Ambition — the deadlock is an opportunity to be seized.
- **Sixth Guard:** King's Recall — the Infernal King revokes the envoy's authority mid-vote → the rogue seat loses all standing, humiliated.
- **Levers:** Legion Writ / Blood-Censure / Gate-Capture
