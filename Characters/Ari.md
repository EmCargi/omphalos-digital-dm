# Ari

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Council of Gatekeepers — Imago's seat (Monad)
**Role:** Infiltrator/wildcard — the gate-engineer who built the Ikarion gate
**Age:** 36

## Appearance

Pale from server-room light, Brainscape contacts glinting, a Monad badge clipped to a jumpsuit — but at Omphalos, the jumpsuit carries a Council seal. The only seat who arrived *through* a virtual gate.

## Personality

Precise, withdrawn, mission-bound. Built the Ikarion gate — the VR game section that's actually a wayline to Ikaris. At Omphalos he's the strangest seat: the one who knows the Cosmic Web can be *engineered*, not just guarded.

## Background

A Monad senior engineer who found the wayline by accident and quietly built the gate around it. When Imago's leaders learned he was a Key, they sent him to take the seat — an engineer holding a throne, and a wildcard the rogue seat can't read.

## Abilities

VR-interface (Brainscape deep), Gate-sense (Ikarion/Ikaris), Way-Bound (gatekeeper). Techniques: Steady Hand, Evasion. Skills: Technical 3, Academic 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Engineer's Burden — the gate is his creation and his wound.
- **Sixth Guard:** Gate-Sever — forced to sever the Ikarion wayline → he destroys the game-world he built.
- **Levers:** Code-Read / Gate-Step / Server-Veil
