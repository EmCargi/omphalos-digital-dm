# Lyra Vance

**Race:** Homo Psyche — hereditary gatekeeper of Enid
**Faction:** Council of Gatekeepers — Enid's seat
**Role:** Moral thrall — the pressured vote
**Age:** 27

## Appearance

Soft-spoken, steady hands, the kind of person who stands between you and the wind. Wears the pale Noa Enid support mantle with a gate-key etched on the sternum. At Omphalos, she is the youngest seat — and the most pressured.

## Personality

Empathic, self-sacrificing to a fault. Will take your wound into herself before she lets you fall. The hereditary gatekeeper's secret weighs on her: she knows where Enid's hurricane gate is, what Keys can do, and that every deployment to "secure an unimportant ruin" is a gate mission in disguise.

## Background

Hereditary gatekeeper of Enid — the Neo-Logos chief scientist's quiet heir. At the deadlocked Council, the rogue seat pressures her to trade Enid's vote for the gestalt's protection. The party's moral choice: does she hold her seat true, or sell it to survive?

## Abilities

Sixth Sense (World Gates), Healing 2, Exorcism 1, Way-Bound (gatekeeper). Techniques: Steady Hand. Skills: Academic 2, Domestic 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** Empathic Hemorrhage — ally's pain floods her; she overextends to siphon it.
- **Sixth Guard:** Soul Exhaustion — ally enters death check → collapses sharing the wound.
- **Levers:** Harmonic Ward / Pain Siphon / Gate Resonance
