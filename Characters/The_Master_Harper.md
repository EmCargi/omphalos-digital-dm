# The Master Harper

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Council of Gatekeepers — Ikaris' seat (College of Harpers)
**Role:** Prime-faithful — the gatekeeper of Ikaris
**Age:** 70s

## Appearance

Silver-haired, harp always within reach, robes stitched with the Vows of Azar's runes. The College of Harpers' Master — and the hidden gatekeeper of Ikaris, heir to the last Emperor's harpist.

## Personality

Measured, wise, unflappable. The gatekeeper tradition passed to the Harpers; the Master Harper tracks every gate on Ikaris — the Aradia portals in each Seven Stars city, the sealed Bazaroth Keys. The deadlock frustrates the Harper most: without Earth's vote, nothing moves.

## Background

Inherited the Master Harper's seat and the gatekeeper's secret. Has read the waylines for decades, advised Legates, watched the Council deadlock. At Omphalos, the Harper is the anchor of order — the one who believes the chamber can still work.

## Abilities

Sixth Sense (World Gates), Way-Bound (gatekeeper), Storm-song (harp). Techniques: Judge Opponent, Steady Hand. Skills: Sacred 3, Academic 3.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Steward's Patience — order is a duty, not a preference.
- **Sixth Guard:** Concordat Break — a gate is destroyed/sealed against the Council → the Harper's faith in the chamber shatters, and the seat goes silent.
- **Levers:** Harper's Writ / Gate-Trace / Chord of Order
