# The Council of Gatekeepers

**Members:** The seven gatekeepers of the Prime Worlds + Earth
**Meeting place:** The Chamber of Omphalos — seven doors, seven thrones
**Current state:** Deadlocked — Earth's gatekeeper (Ozymandius) missing for centuries

The gatekeepers are the self-proclaimed masters of the Multiverse. Their power comes from knowledge of Omphalos and the gates/Keys. The missing Earth gatekeeper — the traditional leader with the deciding vote — has left the Council unable to act. **Rogue members, such as Bazaroth, grow more powerful in the vacuum.**

**Cast hook:** The Master Harper (Ikaris) is the Prime-faithful; the Infernal King's envoy (Bazaroth) is the rogue; Lyra Vance (Enid) is the pressured vote.
