# The Empty Throne — Ozymandius

**Seat:** Earth's throne — the traditional leader of the Council of Omphalos
**Status:** Empty — the gatekeeper has been missing for centuries
**What was lost:** The deciding vote, the location of many Keys to Earth's gates

According to legend, the last gatekeeper of Earth was a powerful entity — perhaps a mage of near-divine power — known as **Ozymandius**. They have been missing for thousands of years. Today Earth has no known gatekeeper and no known ruler; most of Earth's people are ignorant of the Multiverse.

The empty throne is the campaign's open question: is Ozymandius dead, trapped, or gone of their own accord? And who — or what — will take the seat?

**Cast hook:** The lore node — the party confronts the empty throne and learns the deciding vote is the whole game.
