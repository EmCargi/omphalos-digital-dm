# The Bazaroth Rogue Seat

**Member:** The Infernal King (or his envoy) — Bazaroth's seat on the Council
**Doctrine:** Probe weak worlds; don't openly challenge the strong Primes
**In Omphalos:** His power grows in the deadlock — the deciding-vote vacuum benefits him

The Infernal King sits on the Council of Omphalos, though he sometimes sends a trusted demon lord as ambassador. Unlike most worlds, the hellspawn are perfectly aware of other dimensions — and claim destiny to conquer them. In the deadlocked Council, Bazaroth's rogue member is the one actively maneuvering.

**Cast hook:** The antagonist — the rogue seat forces a vote at the climax (`node_05_rogue_seat`). Tie to Disc 9 (`besm_bazaroth`).
